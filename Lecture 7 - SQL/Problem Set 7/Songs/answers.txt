Through the queries, I noticed that songs with higher energy and danceability
scores tended to dominate the top charts. Spotify could use similar audio feature
data to recommend songs that match a user's listening patterns — for instance,
suggesting high-tempo tracks to someone who frequently plays energetic music.
However, this approach has limitations: it ignores lyrical content, cultural
context, and the social factors that influence what people enjoy. A song
recommended purely on audio similarity might feel technically similar but
emotionally mismatched to the listener.
